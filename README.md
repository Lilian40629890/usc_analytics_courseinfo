## Course details

### ISE 521 — Foundations of Analytics

**Instructor:** Bruce Wilcox · **Term:** Fall 2025

#### Topics covered

- Python fundamentals: control flow, lists, tuples, sets, and dictionaries
- Data manipulation with NumPy and pandas; outlier detection (z-score, IQR)
- Hypothesis testing: one-, two-, and multi-sample tests (t-test, z-test, ANOVA, chi-square)
- Confidence intervals and bootstrapping
- Relational data modeling and SQL: joins, aggregation, subqueries, and window functions

### ISE 529 — Predictive Analytics

**Instructor:** Tao Ma · **Term:** Fall 2025

#### Topics covered

- Probability review: random variables, common distributions, and Bayes' formula
- Linear and polynomial regression; inference on coefficients
- Classification: logistic regression and LDA
- Model selection and regularization: cross-validation, ridge, lasso, and PCA
- Tree-based methods and support vector machines
- Neural networks, including RNNs and LSTMs

### ISE 530 — Optimization Methods for Analytics

**Instructor:** Andres Gomez · **Term:** Spring 2026

#### Topics covered

- Unconstrained optimization: optimality conditions, convexity, steepest descent, and Newton's method
- Constrained optimization: Lagrange multipliers and KKT conditions
- Duality: weak and strong duality, duality gap
- Linear programming and the simplex method
- Mixed-integer programming: branch and bound and branching strategies

### ISE 543 — Enterprise Business Intelligence and Systems Analytics

**Instructor:** Bruce Wilcox · **Term:** Spring 2026

#### Topics covered

- Tableau fundamentals: dimensions and measures, discrete and continuous fields, hierarchies
- Combining data in Tableau: unions, joins, and relationships
- Calculations: row-level, aggregate, parameters, and table calculations
- Cloud computing concepts: SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS
- BigQuery and Vertex AI, including AutoML and the Python client library
- MLOps and model explainability (SHAP)
